We stand at the edge of a moment that demands more than silence. The challenges before us are not abstractions debated in distant chambers; they are the lived reality of every person who rises before dawn, who works without rest, who gives more than they receive and still finds themselves asking: is this enough? The answer must be yes. Not because the world has given us permission, but because we have decided it will be. History does not move on its own. It moves when ordinary people refuse the ordinary limits placed upon them. It moves when a voice rises in a room where no voice was expected, and that voice says: we are here, we have always been here, and we will not be silent. So I ask you not for your applause but for your resolve. Not for your agreement in this room but for your action in the world. Because the measure of this moment will not be what we said here today. It will be what we did with what we knew, when knowing was no longer enough.
