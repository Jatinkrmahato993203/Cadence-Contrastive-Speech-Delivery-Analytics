There is a kind of courage that does not announce itself. It does not carry a banner or demand recognition. It moves quietly through the world, choosing again and again to do what is right even when doing what is right is the harder path. We are living through a time that asks for exactly that kind of courage. Not the courage of grand gestures made before crowds, though we need those too, but the courage of small sustained choices made when no one is watching. The courage to speak a true thing in a room full of comfortable agreement. The courage to remain steady when everything around you invites panic or retreat. I have seen this courage in the people I know and the people I have met along the way. It does not belong to any one generation or place or time. It is available to all of us, right now, in whatever room we occupy. The question is simply this: will you reach for it? Not someday. Not when the conditions improve. Not when someone else goes first. Now. Today. Here.
